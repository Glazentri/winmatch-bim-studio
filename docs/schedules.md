# Window and door schedule designer

The designer produces a custom, print-ready schedule from the current opening register, including local user-edited metadata. It does not generate native Revit/Archicad schedules or fabrication drawings.

1. Open **Sheet designer**.
2. Enter a title, company, revision and issue status.
3. Choose the accent colour; upload an approved PNG/JPEG/WebP logo (under 2 MB) or use the company name.
4. Select A4 or A3, portrait or landscape, and card or table layout.
5. Group by level, category or no grouping. Choose metric or imperial display.
6. Toggle schematic elevations. Enable/disable and reorder fields, including model/opening/actual sizes, subsill dimensions, and m²/lm quantities, to suit the audience (design, client selections or supplier pack).
7. Preview the complete schedule. Select Print / save PDF and use the browser PDF printer. Select matching page size/orientation and disable browser-added headers/footers if needed.
8. Save the sheet template to JSON. Import it in a future project to reuse branding and field selections.

The sheet template includes the optional logo as embedded image data. Share only when the logo and branding may be redistributed. A sheet template is separate from the BIM snapshot.

The nominal opening sizes originate from the host and are not changed in Studio. Schematic elevations depict selected operation only, not detailed frame profiles, glazing bars, handing, opening clearance, structural opening or fabrication tolerances.
